Hoods & ventilation, sized to the line

Ventilation is the piece that most often stalls a permit. We spec it from your actual cooking equipment, not a rule of thumb.

Type I hood

Grease and smoke

Goes over cooking that throws off grease-laden vapor and heat, and needs fire suppression inside the hood.

  • Ranges, griddles, fryers, charbroilers, smokers
  • Stainless hood, grease filters and ductwork
  • Roof-mounted upblast exhaust fan
  • Fire suppression and sprinklers per local code
Type II hood

Heat and steam

Goes over equipment that gives off heat, moisture or combustion by-products, but not grease or smoke.

  • Dishwashing machines
  • Steamers, kettles and pasta cookers
  • Condensate removal where required
  • Requirements vary by jurisdiction — we confirm locally
  • Hood
  • Grease filters
  • Ductwork
  • Upblast fan
  • Make-up air
  • Heater

Some equipment, such as pizza and bakery ovens, may be treated differently by your local fire and building officials, so the final call always follows your jurisdiction’s code.

Four stages, start to finish. You'll know what's happening at each one before it starts.
